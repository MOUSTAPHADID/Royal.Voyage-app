# Payment Settlement Terms

**Effective Date**: April 25, 2026

## 1. Settlement Cycle

Settlements are processed monthly on the 15th of each month for the previous calendar month.

## 2. Settlement Components

Each settlement includes:

- **Gross Sales**: Total booking amounts
- **Commissions**: Percentage or fixed amount per agreement
- **Markup**: Additional fees collected
- **Wallet Adjustments**: Prepayments and withdrawals
- **Credit Adjustments**: Credit usage and limits
- **Net Amount**: Amount due to or from Provider

## 3. Settlement Reports

Providers can access settlement reports through the Provider Portal:

- Detailed transaction breakdown
- Commission calculations
- Markup details
- Wallet and credit movements
- Export to CSV or PDF

## 4. Payment Methods

Royal Voyage processes settlements via:

- Bank transfer
- Credit to Provider's wallet
- Check (for large amounts)

## 5. Payment Timing

- Settlements are calculated on the 15th
- Payments are processed within 5 business days
- Bank transfers typically arrive within 2-3 business days

## 6. Disputes

Providers may dispute settlements within 30 days by submitting:

- Specific transaction details
- Supporting documentation
- Explanation of discrepancy

Royal Voyage will investigate and respond within 15 business days.

## 7. Minimum Settlement

Settlements below $100 USD equivalent are held until the next month.

## 8. Late Payments

If Provider owes Royal Voyage:

- Payment is due within 30 days
- Late payments incur 1.5% monthly interest
- Continued non-payment may result in service suspension
